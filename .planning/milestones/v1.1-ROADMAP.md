## Objective

Deixar o backend completamente preparado para o início do Frontend Milestone 1, eliminando dependências backend ainda abertas e entregando Store API, contratos, persistência, segurança, testes e artefatos de handoff suficientes para que o frontend possa começar sem inventar endpoints, regras ou schemas.

## Immutable inheritance from v1.0

- `Order` only after canonical Stripe webhook confirmation;
- durable backend `purchase_completed` outbox;
- Gelato only after confirmed `Order` plus durable local event;
- downstream provider failures do not change financial truth;
- Pix negative states never create `Order`;
- refunds become financial truth only after reliable Stripe webhook;
- no frontend flow creates `Order`;
- secrets, raw CPF, opaque tokens, `client_secret` and Pix payloads stay out of logs;
- v1.0 tag/Release/archive remain immutable.
