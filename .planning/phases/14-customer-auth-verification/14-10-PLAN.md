## Refresh protocol — P14-D06/P14-D07

Opaque refresh de 32 bytes base64url; DB guarda SHA-256. Transaction locks N, valida active/inactivity/lineage/version/deadline, consome N e cria deterministicamente N+1 hash/nonce/generation/replacement. Recovery exige o mesmo N, mesmo request-key hash, <=45s e N+1 unused; rederiva o mesmo capability. Chave diferente, >45s, descendant used ou malformed consumed token revoga lineage/descendants. JWT contém customer/identity, `sid`, `cv`, `jti`, `iat`,`exp=min(now+10m,absolute)`.
