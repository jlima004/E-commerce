## Verification state machine — P14-D08

`pending(N) -> claimed -> confirmed`; resend sob row lock supersede pending/claimed anteriores e cria N+1+outbox na mesma transaction. Confirm usa conditional update hash+pending+expiry, grava `email_verified_at`, confirma vencedora e supersede demais. Provider exhaustion afeta somente outbox; intent permanece pending até TTL/resend. Native route/event/provider_metadata não participa.
